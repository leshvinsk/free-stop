# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Our standards

**Expected behaviour:**
- Be respectful and constructive in all discussions
- Give and receive feedback graciously
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community
- Assume good intent from other contributors

**Unacceptable behaviour:**
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Dismissing or belittling contributions
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other community channel associated with Free Stop.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported by contacting the maintainer directly through GitHub. All reports will be reviewed and handled with discretion.

Maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, close issues, or ban contributors who violate this Code of Conduct.
